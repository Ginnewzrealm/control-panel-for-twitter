# Privacy Policy

Control Panel for Twitter · 宽屏私人版 does not collect, sell, or share personal information or browsing activity with its developer or with developer-operated services.

The extension runs on X/Twitter pages to apply user-selected interface and layout changes. To provide these features, it may locally read or modify X/Twitter page requests and responses, and selected features may make requests to X/Twitter APIs using the user's existing X/Twitter session. This data is not sent to the developer or to any developer-operated service, and is not retained by the extension beyond the current browser session.

Extension preferences are stored locally on the user's device with `chrome.storage.local`.

### Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the [project's GitHub repository](https://github.com/Ginnewzrealm/control-panel-for-twitter/issues).
